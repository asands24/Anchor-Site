# Security & Compliance

Trust is the foundation of Anchor. We handle your customer data with the highest standard of care.

## Data Privacy
- **Zero Retention (Optional)**: Configure Anchor to process requests transiently without storing conversation logs.
- **PII Redaction**: Automatic detection and redaction of PII before sending data to LLMs.
- **Tenancy**: Logical isolation for Standard plans; Physical isolation for Enterprise plans.

## Compliance Posture
- **SOC 2 Type II**: In progress (Audit window completes Q3 2026).
- **GDPR**: Fully compliant. Data processing addendum (DPA) available.
- **HIPAA**: BAA available for Enterprise plans.

## Infrastructure Security
- **Encryption**: AES-256 at rest, TLS 1.3 in transit.
- **Access Control**: Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) for your team dashboard.
- **SSO**: SAML V2.0 and OIDC support (Okta, Google, Azure AD).

## Sub-processors
We use specific third-party vendors for specialized capabilities.
- **Vector Database**: Pinecone (US-East)
- **LLM Providers**: OpenAI, Anthropic (Zero-retention agreements in place)
