# Anchor Overview

Anchor is an AI-powered support copilot designed to modernize enterpise customer service operations without replacing your existing infrastructure.

## What is Anchor?
Anchor sits between your customers and your support team, acting as an intelligent first line of defense. It ingests your public documentation, help center articles, and internal knowledge base to provide instant, accurate answers to customer inquiries.

## Key Capabilities
- **Instant Triage**: Automatically categorizes and prioritizes incoming tickets.
- **Auto-Resolution**: Resolves up to 40% of L1 support queries without human intervention.
- **Agent Assist**: Drafts responses for human agents to review, reducing handling time by 60%.
- **Knowledge Gap Analysis**: Identifies missing or outdated documentation based on customer questions.

## Who is Anchor For?
- **High-Volume Support Teams**: Scaling support without scaling headcount.
- **Technical Products**: Where support queries require deep product knowledge.
- **Enterprise SaaS**: Where security and data privacy are non-negotiable.

## The Anchor Difference
Unlike generic chatbots, Anchor is:
1. **Context-Aware**: Understands user history and account details (if configured).
2. **Secure by Design**: Tunable privacy controls and data retention policies.
3. **Frontend Agnostic**: Works as a widget, an API, or integrated into your existing helpdesk.
