# F.A.Q.

Common questions from our prospective customers.

## Pricing & Licensing
**Q: Do you charge by seat or by conversation?**
A: We charge primarily by "resolved conversations." We believe you shouldn't pay for questions that weren't answered effectively. There is a small base platform fee for indexing.

**Q: Is there a free trial?**
A: Yes, we offer a 14-day full-feature trial with a limit of 1,000 conversations.

## Technical
**Q: How long does initial indexing take?**
A: For a typical docs site (500 pages), indexing takes less than 10 minutes.

**Q: Can I bring my own LLM key?**
A: On Enterprise plans, yes. You can use your own Azure OpenAI or Bedrock credentials.

## Support
**Q: What happens if the AI gives a wrong answer?**
A: You can flag it in the dashboard. You can also "pin" correct answers to specific queries to override the AI for future requests.

**Q: Do you offer SLAs?**
A: Yes, Enterprise plans come with a 99.9% uptime SLA and 1-hour support response time.
